# Data source policy

## Historical candles
Primary downloader: https://github.com/Leo4815162342/dukascopy-node

The app does not claim Dukascopy candles are the one official FX price. Spot FX is OTC; different providers can show small differences. Source/provider must remain visible in the manifest.

## Regulatory / risk facts
CFTC retail OTC forex advisory:
https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/CustomerAdvisory_MustKnowForex.html

## Macro-event lessons
Prefer primary-source archives:
- Federal Reserve: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm
- U.S. BLS: https://www.bls.gov/bls/newsrels.htm
- ECB: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/govcdec/mopo/html/index.en.html
- Bank of England: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-summary-and-minutes
- Bank of Japan: https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmsche_minu/index.htm

## Prohibited shortcuts
- No fabricated candles labeled as historical.
- No unsourced historical-event narratives presented as fact.
- No signal language such as “RSI 70 = sell.”
- No performance guarantee.
